Schedulers:
- Beta Schedules (beta_t): Determines how much new Gaussian noise is added in a single forward step (x_{t-1} -> x_t). 
Linear and sigmoid stay bounded within [beta_start, beta_end] <-> cosine rises sharply near the end because signal alpha_bar approaches zero at t -> T.
- Alpha Schedules (alpha_t): Represents how much of existing signal is kept per step (higher alpha = less new noise added). 
Keeping higher alpha early and midway (as in cosine) preserves image structure longer, supporting smoother denoising and better sample quality.
